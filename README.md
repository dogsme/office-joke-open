One line of office comedy for [Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open](https://make.fun/bounty/u-aldy998-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-the-office-joke-open-9920f232) on make.fun — a single joke, at most 200 characters, judged head to head by an AI judge.

## The entry

`main.py` writes one joke to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Our 15-minute standup kept running over, so management scheduled a weekly meeting to discuss shortening it — it's now in its third week.

The joke is about the meeting that could have been an email, taken one step further: the meeting about the meeting, now in its third week.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The output is a single UTF-8 line, well under the 200-character limit.
